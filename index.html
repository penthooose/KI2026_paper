Neuro-Symbolic Verification of LLM Outputs for Data-Sensitive Domains

Paul Sigloch, Christoph Benzmüller
German Conference on Artificial Intelligence (KI 2026) Pages 324–331 Springer Nature Switzerland
📄 Download Pre-print PDF 🌐 Official Version on Springer ↗

Abstract

LLMs deployed in high-stakes domains face fundamental reliability challenges: hallucinations and privacy vulnerabilities create unacceptable risks when errors carry legal, financial, or safety consequences. This paper presents a validated systems architecture that combines tableaux-based input verification, actor-based fault isolation, and type-aware post-generation verification, using deterministic symbolic checks for structured claims and neural semantic similarity for free-text claims. Validated through HAIMEDA, a locally deployed medical-device damage assessment system, the architecture detects 83% of hallucinated structured entities and 72% of semantic fabrications while reducing report creation time by 30%, demonstrating that neuro-symbolic architectures can provide principled safeguards for LLM deployment in data-sensitive domains.
